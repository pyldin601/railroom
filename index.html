About the sounds

The electric motor is synthesized continuously from speed and throttle, with layered harmonics and gentle FM. Wheel, rolling and braking sounds use public-domain and CC0 recordings from different trains. It is not a recording of the Kyiv–Fastiv service. The rolling bed is filtered from one ride; the braking layer is recorded electric deceleration. Pneumatic brake hiss and independent speed-band recordings are not included.

Welds are subtle. The jointed demo has a stronger clatter. Fastenings do not trigger impacts.
